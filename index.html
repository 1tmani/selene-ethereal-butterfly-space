Trust matters

Good connections begin with safety.

Marriage is personal. Your profile should never feel like public property. The production platform should include verification, reporting, blocking, profile review and clear privacy controls.

  • Never share OTPs, bank details or money with a match.
  • Meet for the first time in a public place and tell someone your plans.
  • Report pressure, impersonation, harassment or suspicious financial requests.
  • Contact details should stay hidden until the member grants permission.
